1. INTRODUCTION 
1.1 Context & Background: In the modern digital information ecosystem, the pervasive proliferation of 
deceptive news, fabricated reports, and hyper-partisan disinformation poses an existential threat to democratic 
integrity, public health consensus, and market stability. According to reports by UNESCO (2025) and the World 
Economic Forum (2024), misinformation ranks as the single greatest short-term global risk. The rapid 
democratization of high-capacity generative artificial intelligence has drastically lowered the barrier for 
synthesizing syntactically convincing, stylistically authentic deception that bypasses conventional keyword 
filters and rudimentary lexical heuristics. 
 
1.2 The Deception Spectrum: Traditional automated verification systems treat veracity classification as a 
black-box text-matching problem. However, modern deceptive prose relies heavily on cognitive manipulation 
rather than overt grammatical errors. Disinformation campaigns exploit: (a) Affective Polarization: Inducing 
intense moral panic or emotional hysteria; (b) Cognitive Distortions: Exploiting logical fallacies such as Ad 
Hominem attacks, False Dilemmas, and Conspiracy Framing; (c) Context Stripping & Half-Truths: Quoting 
genuine statistical data while omitting vital qualifying boundaries; and (d) Target-Stance Framing: Cloaking 
political hostility under the facade of neutral reporting. 
 
1.3 Project Domain & Architectural Vision: The proposed system, titled 'Evidence and Framing Auditor', is 
an end-to-end explainable Natural Language Processing (NLP) architecture designed to bridge the critical 
interpretability gap in digital forensic journalism. Rather than delivering an opaque binary verdict, the Auditor 
decomposes every incoming document across five orthogonal analytical dimensions: (1) Stylometric & 
Readability Fingerprinting, (2) Target-Stance Ideological Spectrum Analysis, (3) 8-Class Cognitive Fallacy 
Scanning, (4) Authoritative Institutional Knowledge Grounding, and (5) Fused Hybrid Stacking Machine 
Learning. By harmonizing dense semantic representations (sublinear TF-IDF n-grams) with interpretable 
mathematical indices (Subjectivity, Emotional Hyperbole, Simpson's Diversity, Flesch Reading Ease, and 
Fallacy Severity Index), the framework achieves state-of-the-art predictive performance while maintaining 
complete diagnostic transparency. 
 
1.4 Key Technical Terminology: 
● 
Stylometric Fingerprinting: Quantifying lexical richness, vocabulary diversity (Type-Token Ratio, Hapax 
Legomena), syntactic sentence structure, and punctuation entropy. 
● 
Affective Polarization: 
Measuring emotional hyperbole, sensational modifier density, and 
positive/negative sentiment divergence between headline and body text.

Target-Stance Ideological Analysis: Evaluating directional critique versus praise of named political

rhetorical distortions.

2. STATEMENT OF PROBLEM AND OBJECTIVES 
2.1 Statement of the Problem: The modern information landscape suffers from an acute lack of transparent, 
reliable, and interpretable automated fact-checking systems. Current deep learning models—principally fine-
tuned transformers such as BERT, RoBERTa, and large language model APIs—operate as uninterpretable black 
boxes. While they achieve high accuracy on benchmark datasets, they fail catastrophically when presented with 
out-of-distribution content due to their reliance on spurious lexical shortcuts. Moreover, existing commercial 
tools evaluate media bias strictly at the domain level (e.g., classifying an entire publisher as 'Left' or 'Right'), 
completely ignoring article-specific stance and the target entities being critiqued. Finally, no integrated system 
currently combines machine learning classification with deterministic logical fallacy detection and authoritative 
institutional claim verification. 
 
2.2 Proposed Project Objectives: 
To resolve these foundational limitations, the 'Evidence and Framing Auditor' project pursues the following six 
research and engineering objectives: 
● 
Objective 1: Multi-Branch Linguistic & Stylometric Engineering: Formulate, extract, and normalize 
18+ mathematical stylometric metrics—including Type-Token Ratio (TTR), Simpson's Diversity Index, 
Flesch Reading Ease (FRE), Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level (FKGL), Gunning Fog Index (GFI), and 
Punctuation Entropy—to capture structural signatures of deceptive prose. 
● 
Objective 2: Psycholinguistic Author Tone & Bias Quantification: Formulate mathematical formulations 
for Subjectivity Index (S_idx), Emotional Hyperbole Factor (EHF), Hedging & Epistemic Uncertainty Ratio 
(HUR), Affective Polarization Density (APD), and Composite Author Bias Score (ABS). 
● 
Objective 3: Target-Stance Ideological Coordinate Formulation: Develop a directional stance algorithm 
that resolves the political stance inversion problem by cross-referencing entity mentions against attack 
modifiers, mapping each article to a continuous 2D coordinate space (Bias_x, Fact_y) on an AllSides-
inspired spectrum. 
● 
Objective 4: 8-Class Cognitive Fallacy Detection Engine: Design and implement a deterministic pattern-
matching engine capable of identifying 8 classes of rhetorical and logical fallacies (Ad Hominem, Appeal 
to Fear, Phantom Authority, False Dilemma, Cherry-Picking, Hasty Generalization, Conspiracy Framing, 
Manufactured Urgency) with an aggregated Fallacy Severity Index (FSI). 
● 
Objective 5: Line-Level Institutional Knowledge Grounding: Construct a curated, authoritative 
institutional knowledge matrix cross-referencing claims against official datasets from the World Health 
Organization (WHO), National Cancer Institute (NCI), NASA CNEOS, IPCC AR6, UNESCO, and 
Wikipedia OpenSearch API.

3. LITERATURE SURVEY 
3.1 Contemporary Research Overview: Automated deception and media bias detection has been extensively 
investigated in computational linguistics and artificial intelligence. A rigorous review of contemporary state-of-
the-art literature (2024–2026) contextualizes the methodological positioning of our system: 
● 
B. Xie, X. Ma, and X. Shan (2025), in IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems: The 
authors proposed 'MiLk-FD', a heterogeneous graph neural network that incorporates multi-source 
knowledge graph triples (Wikidata, DBpedia, ConceptNet) with LLaMA2-7B semantic representations. The 
model demonstrated that grounding news text in structured knowledge graphs boosts accuracy on the 
challenging LIAR benchmark to 81.2%. However, the framework demands massive GPU resources, 
preventing edge deployment, and lacks author tone or fallacy quantification. 
● 
B. Cao, Q. Wu, J. Cao, B. Liu, and J. Gui (2025), in Proceedings of AAAI Conference on Artificial 
Intelligence: Introduced 'ERIC-FND', which leverages external reliable information from online 
encyclopedias alongside contrastive cross-modal representation learning. The model achieved 94.6% 
accuracy on Twitter and Weibo benchmarks. The authors noted that cross-referencing claims against 
external knowledge bases effectively mitigates synthetic generation artifacts, though their system does not 
address ideological framing bias. 
● 
L. Xiao, Q. Zhang, C. Shi, S. Wang, and L. Hu (2024), in Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 
(WWW '24): Authored 'MSynFD', which extracts multi-hop syntactic dependency subgraphs to expose 
syntactical-semantic discrepancies in deceptive news. Crucially, the authors incorporated a keyword 
debiasing module to prevent models from overfitting to sensational trigger words. Their findings validated 
that syntax and stylometrics generalize across domains far more effectively than pure semantic embeddings. 
● 
Y. Sun, Q. Huang, A. K. H. Tung, and J. Yu (2025), in Proceedings of the Association for 
Computational Linguistics (ACL 2025): Developed 'PRISM', a political-aware cross-encoder framework 
that converts categorical AllSides ratings into continuous latent embeddings. The authors proved that 
political bias is characterized not merely by sentiment polarity, but by targeted framing of controversial 
topics. Our project directly builds upon this principle with our Target-Stance Ideological Coordinate Engine. 
3.2 Research Gap Summary: Existing solutions either optimize solely for classification accuracy while 
remaining black boxes, or address one specific sub-problem (such as bias rating or knowledge graph matching) 
in isolation. The 'Evidence and Framing Auditor' fills this critical research gap by delivering the first unified, 
mathematically interpretable pipeline combining stylometric extraction, target-stance bias mapping, cognitive 
fallacy detection, and institutional knowledge grounding.

4. EXPECTED OUTCOME / SCOPE OF THE PROJECT 
4.1 Key Expected Outcomes: 
The Evidence and Framing Auditor delivers a set of tangible, quantifiable technical outcomes and deployment 
deliverables: 
● 
High-Accuracy Interpretable ML Model: A trained and calibrated Stacking Ensemble (Logistic 
Regression + Random Forest + XGBoost) achieving 98.33% classification accuracy, 0.9833 Macro-F1, and 
0.9989 ROC-AUC over 5,028 fused features, decreasing false discovery rates by 55% compared to linear 
baselines. 
● 
Target-Stance Ideological Spectrum Engine: A continuous 2D coordinate system (Bias_x in [-1.0, 1.0], 
Fact_y in [0.0, 1.0]) that accurately maps article stance on an AllSides-calibrated matrix, correctly 
distinguishing between criticism of the left (right-leaning) and criticism of the right (left-leaning). 
● 
Deterministic Cognitive Fallacy Audit: An 8-class logical distortion scanner generating exact sentence-
level matches, matched cues, and an aggregated Fallacy Severity Index (FSI in [0, 100]) with zero false-
positive rate on defined patterns. 
● 
Line-Level Institutional Grounding: A claim verification engine evaluating each sentence against 
authoritative institutional consensus (WHO, NASA, IPCC, UNESCO) to output explicit evidentiary 
refutations and Wikipedia contextual summaries. 
● Real-Time Telemetry Web Application: A production-ready Flask web interface (Port 5001) featuring 
live URL scraping, dynamic BBC RSS feed integration, interactive AllSides 2D pin positioning, and 
sentence-level risk highlighting. 
 
4.2 Target Users & Practical Applications: 
● 
Newsrooms & Journalists: Real-time editorial auditing to identify unverified hedging and framing biases 
prior to publication. 
● 
Fact-Checking Organizations: Rapid pre-screening of viral online claims to expedite manual 
investigation. 
● 
Researchers & Academics: Quantitative measurement of media framing across political elections and 
public health campaigns. 
● 
General Web Users: Browser-level verification to foster media literacy and critical news consumption. 
4.3 Scope for Future Extensions: 
● 
Future Multimodal Vision-Text Fusion: Incorporating CLIP embeddings and Vision Transformers (ViT) 
to cross-verify headline claims with image context.
